# Starter

Got it from Ines at the market in March, in a jam jar. She said it was eleven years old.

It lives on the second shelf of the fridge now. I take it out Friday night and feed it, and by Saturday morning it has doubled if the kitchen is warm. If it hasn't doubled I wait. Waiting is most of it.

The levain is what actually goes into the dough, not the starter itself. I keep forgetting that and using the starter straight, and the loaf comes out flat.

A starter older than a year makes a sourer loaf. That's why Ines's one is so good.

Feeding: one part starter, one part flour, one part water, by weight. 50g each. On weekdays it stays in the fridge and I feed it on Wednesday too, or it goes grey on top and smells like nail polish. Grey liquid on top means it's hungry, pour it off. Rye flour wakes it up faster than white. When I was away in May it went three weeks without a feed and came back after two days of feeding twice a day. I tried 1:2:2 once, it was slower but the bread was milder. In summer I might move it to 1:5:5 so it doesn't overshoot by morning.

Autolyse helps too but that's a different thing.
