# The evening class

A year of watercolours on Tuesdays, 20 a week, and I still cannot draw a hand without erasing it three times.

I keep doing the same arithmetic with it: what I paid against what came back. Then I call the year a failure and say I should have known.

The part I never write down is that I go every Tuesday anyway.
